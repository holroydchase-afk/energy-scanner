# UX review, round 4: Energy Scanner, showcase mode (2026-10-23)

I'm the project's ux-reviewer. This round covers the sprint build (PR #16, branch `m2.14-ui-review`), Chase's iPhone shots of the live site, and the UI code. It was a read-only review: no repo files were changed.

**Build reviewed:** committed `HEAD` (1a277e3). That is the m2.5 to m2.13 stack, merged into the PR #16 branch.

**Note:** the mask refinement (`src/segment/refine.ts`) was written in the same session while this review ran; ES-R21 says how it relates.

## Personas (least tech-savvy first)
1. A tired parent at bedtime with a 5-year-old (the product's own user), on an iPhone.
2. A non-technical recruiter on a phone with 90 seconds.
3. A hiring manager on a laptop with 5 minutes, who may also read the code on GitHub.

## Verdict: **Needs changes**
- **Parent (iPhone):** the palm flow works. Their own photos can render broken-looking:
  - square pixel tiles at the edges
  - a dark jagged line around fingers
  - a whitewashed room
  - a second child or a ceiling fan caught in the aura
  
  Two more problems: ticked steps reset at 10 PM on the US West Coast, and a bad file shows its error off-screen.
- **Recruiter (phone):** the first screen is clear, and Try the sample works. With Always Grey on by default, though, the sample shows a grey "white smoke" aura, the weakest look. Getting to the colors takes 4 steps.
- **Hiring manager (laptop):** the app looks finished, but after a scan the reading card sits below the fold. On GitHub, `result.ts` still ships the v1 energy-type chips and slider with adult horoscope copy ("You walk in and the room notices"). That contradicts the kids' story.

**No regressions of ES-R1 to R20.** Checked in the screenshots:
- tagline, footer links, 44px targets, text 14px or larger, bedtime line on one line
- the scan switch on one line at 320px
- the Always Grey note, "Still to do", the not-an-image copy
- no horizontal scroll, and only benign GL warnings in `log.txt`

**Already fixed in PR #16** (seen in the live iPhone shots of the older build, not re-reported):
- "To drain the battery" copy
- missing tagline
- monospace "Clear tonight's steps"
- no portfolio link or GitHub link in the footer
- no Try the sample

**Light mode:** not honoured. Light and dark screenshots are pixel-identical (`color-scheme: dark`, `style.css:11`, and no `prefers-color-scheme` rule). That matches the dark-only decision. My recommendation is to keep it: it's a night-time product, and the native controls render dark correctly. Add one line to CASE-STUDY.md so it reads as a choice, not an omission. Not logged as a finding.

## Acceptance criteria (for qa-tdd)

**On device (Chase's iPhone and the three real photos):**
- **AC-R21a, confirm on device:** at 100% zoom on each saved image, no square tiles along the outline, and no dark line wider than 1px between skin and glow.
- **AC-R22b:** on device, the room behind the subject stays recognizable (furniture edges visible).
- **AC-R23b:** on device, the fan and the second person get no glow.
- **AC-R28, confirm on device:** scrolled to the very bottom in Safari, "Code on GitHub" is fully above the toolbar and tappable. The page has no horizontal scroll at 320.

**Unit tests:**
- **AC-R21b:** given a mask with a mid-confidence (0.4–0.6) 8x8-cell checkerboard at the edge, the composite alpha after refinement varies by less than 0.1 between neighboring photo pixels away from luminance edges.
- **AC-R22a:** with intensity 0.7, the background luma at 0.3 short-side units from the outline is at least 35% of the original. The Grey level's `aura.colors[1]` is `#8f98d1`.
- **AC-R23a:** a mask with two blobs (area 10% and 2%, apart by 20% of the short side) produces `field.sdf > 0` everywhere inside the smaller blob.
- **AC-R24:** with Always Grey on, the reading card shows the button "See every color". Clicking it unchecks Always Grey, persists `alwaysGrey=false`, and shows a new reading with the note hidden. With Always Grey off, the button is absent.
- **AC-R29:** the `.btn.primary` gradient stops are `#7c3aed` and `#be185d`, and `background-origin` is `border-box`.
- **AC-R30:** `bedtimeFor(03:20, '20:00')` returns the previous day's 20:00. A scan at 03:20 with Always Grey off and 0 steps reads Grey or Blue Moon, never Rainbow.
- **AC-R31:** under `TZ=America/Los_Angeles`, `nightKey` gives the same key at 19:30 and 23:30, and a different key at 05:30 the next day. Also under `TZ=Asia/Tokyo`.
- **AC-R33:** `result.ts` has no `.chip`, `.slider` or `ENERGY_TYPES`. `style.css` has no selectors without a matching class in `src/` (scripted check). `.frame` and `.stage` are each defined once.
- **AC-R34:** after **New photo**, `document.activeElement` is the Take a photo button. `[data-hint]` has `aria-live="polite"`. The range's accessible name contains "Try a different time", and `aria-valuetext` equals the shown text. Emoji spans in the mode buttons are `aria-hidden`.
- **AC-R35:** with reduced motion set, `scrollIntoView` is called with `behavior: 'auto'`. `body` has no `background-attachment: fixed`.

**Playwright:**
- **AC-R25:** at 390x844 and 320x640, after choosing a non-image file, the `.error` box's bounding rect is fully inside the viewport.
- **AC-R26:** at 1280x800, after Try the sample, the photo and the whole `.reading-card` are inside the viewport together.
- **AC-R27:** the vertical gap between the `.caption` bottom and the `.actions` top is 16px or less.
- **AC-R32:** a file dropped on `document.body` outside `#app` at 1280 wide starts a scan, and the URL is unchanged.
