## Personas (draft, confirm with Chase; there's no personas doc in the repo)
Least tech-savvy first:
1. **The kid** (4 to 7, many can't read yet). Guiding text doesn't reach them at all. Only the icons, the big pad and motion do.
2. **The tired parent** at bedtime, with one hand on the phone. They read nothing longer than a button.
3. **The recruiter** on a phone with 90 seconds: what is it, and does it work?
4. **The hiring manager**, harshest critic (from `.claude/design-rules.md`): has seen a hundred AI-built demos.

## Verdict: Needs changes
- Kid: can tap the steps and the hand, but the five emoji and a wall of words compete with the pad. Every hint is written for someone who reads.
- Parent: has to read 70 words before reaching the pad at 390px. "Too fast! Hold until the circle fills" punishes a tap.
- Recruiter: the proof point (on-device AI aura) is there, but buried in sentence two of a 29-word intro.
- Hiring manager: see below.

**Critic's line:** "It reads like someone pasted the README onto the screen. Show me, don't tell me."

**Score:** 4/10 at 390, 4/10 at 1280 (calibrated to Chase). Evidence:
- 390: the first viewport holds 70 visible words before the Scan button, which sits below the fold. That fails the squint test (V1): the reading block competes with the pad.
- Every interaction is narrated twice. The palm scan says what to do (hint), what it's doing ("Scanning… keep holding"), that it finished ("Scan complete"), then repeats the step chips in "Steps left tonight".
- Emoji as icons (V9).
- "Upload a photo" sits under a line promising "Nothing is uploaded".

What the 4 does have: one accent, used only on tappable things; one type family; 44px+ targets everywhere (ux-shots measured it).

**Expected after these fixes:** 8/10 at 390, 7.5/10 at 1280. At 1280 the palm screen keeps a lot of empty canvas either side of the pad; that's a layout pass, not a text pass.

## Design changes that make the text unnecessary
1. **Steps as checkboxes (TXT-R2).** Each step gets a 20px circle in its top-right corner, 8px in from the edges.
   - Unticked: a hollow circle with a 2px `var(--line)` border.
   - Ticked: filled `var(--accent)` with a white Lucide `check` (stroke 3).
   - The tile keeps its 2px accent border when pressed.
   - A hollow circle is the universal "tap to tick" sign, so the label and the count both go.
2. **The pad explains itself (TXT-R3, R4).**
   - Idle: the ring breathes (opacity 0.4 to 1, 2s ease-in-out loop; static with reduced motion). The hand icon invites a touch; the Scan button gives the word.
   - During: the ring fills and the beam sweeps.
   - After: the ring locks to the energy color and the card appears.
   - No status line at all. Live text stays for screen readers.
3. **A tap never fails (TXT-R4, recommended).**
   - A press shorter than the hold turns into a button scan, the same as Scan, so the ring just finishes.
   - Holding still works and feels like the ritual. This removes the only error state on the main screen.
   - Principle 4 is untouched: the Scan button stays.
   - It changes the hold-to-scan mechanic from the original artifact, so it's **Chase's call**. Fallback: keep the hold, and say "Keep holding" (2 words).
4. **One line under the title (TXT-R1).**
   - Today the tagline and pitch are 29 words in two type colors. Make them one muted line, at the same size as the byline.
   - On phones, "See the aura demo" sits on the line below it. At 1280 it sits on the same line.
5. **The photo screen is just buttons (TXT-R6, R7, R8).**
   - Orb, then the buttons:
     - Touch: Take a photo (tinted) and Choose a photo, then a quiet Try the sample.
     - Laptop: Try the sample (tinted) and Choose a photo.
   - The drop hint only appears while you're dragging.
   - The header's demo button hides in photo mode, so the sample has one name.
6. **One line-icon set (TXT-R9): yes, in this pass.**
   - Icons are what lets the step label and the mode words go; emoji are the loudest V9 tell left.
   - **Lucide** (ISC, v1.77.0): 24px grid, 2px stroke, round caps. It's the only set I checked that has every icon we need: `bath`, `shirt`, `toothbrush`, `toilet`, `book-open`, `hand`, `camera`, `check`, and `settings` if wanted (the preview is shot 13 below).
   - Copy those paths into one `src/ui/icons.ts` as inline SVG strings (`fill="none" stroke="currentColor"`, `aria-hidden="true"`). Add the ISC notice to `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, plus `licenses/ISC-lucide.txt`.
   - **No new dependency**, not even a dev one. No CDN.
   - Sizes: 28px in the steps (stroke 2), 20px in the mode switch (stroke 2), 96px on the pad (stroke 1.5, so it doesn't look heavy at that size).
   - Checked against Phosphor (MIT). It also covers every step, but its icons are filled outline paths, so the stroke weight can't match the check and ring strokes.
   - Steps lose the emoji color. The ticked state (accent check plus accent border) carries the color instead.
7. **Progressive disclosure for Grown-ups.** It already stays closed. Inside, the 50-word help shrinks to a 4-word description under the switch, wired with `aria-describedby`. No new disclosure is needed.

**Flags (not applied):**
- TXT-R4 changes the hold mechanic (Chase's call, product story).
- "Automatic" and the shorter labels don't touch the tokens or any principle the code cites.
- STUDIO-CORE.md section 2 isn't in this repo. If it has an "every action is explained in words" principle, TXT-R3 needs Chase's OK. The Scan button keeps a visible word on the pad either way.
- No design tokens change. The breathing ring and the check circle use the existing `--line`, `--accent` and `--energy`.
